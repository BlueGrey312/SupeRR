Mathematical Methodology & Audit Trail

1. Objective Pressure Triggers (Pre-Delivery Classification)

Every delivery is evaluated strictly before the ball is bowled using completed deliveries from the match context. A ball is classified as under pressure if ANY of the following 3 objective criteria hold true:

  • High-Pressure Chase: Second innings, at least 2 wickets already lost, and Required Run Rate ≥ 9.5 runs per over.
  • Late High-Pressure Chase: Second innings, ≤ 30 legal balls remaining, RRR ≥ 8.0, and at least 1 wicket already lost.
  • First-Innings Finishing Pressure: First innings, ≤ 30 legal balls remaining, and at least 3 wickets already lost.

2. Composite Score Formula & Exposure Discount

Each metric is percentile-ranked across all qualifying players, weighted into a raw composite, and multiplied by the exposure reliability factor:

Batters: (0.30 × Press_SR) + (0.22 × SR_Uplift) + (0.15 × Consistency) + (0.13 × Boundary_Rate) + (0.10 × Low_Dismissal_Rate) + (0.10 × Volume)
Bowlers: (0.27 × Low_Econ) + (0.23 × Wicket_Rate) + (0.22 × Econ_Improvement) + (0.10 × Consistency) + (0.10 × Volume) + (0.08 × Dot_Rate)

Final Reliability-Adjusted Score: Raw_Composite × (n / (n + 150))

Where n represents the player's total legal pressure balls in the dataset. This penalizes micro-sample outliers and ensures reliability under war room scrutiny.
